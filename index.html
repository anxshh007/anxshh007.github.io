SOLARIS

Technical Lead

An explainable AI intelligence layer for distributed solar. The SIF-X engine fuses weather, generation, battery, grid and tariff data into a single recommended action — with confidence, drivers and reasoning attached. Covers forecasting, panel-layout optimisation, battery-degradation tracking, dispatch and DISCOM net-metering. Backpropagation hand-derived in NumPy, so it runs offline with no GPU.
